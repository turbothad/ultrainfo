# TUR-8: Follow section

## Objective

Add an event-generic Follow section showing start, turnaround cutoff, and final cutoff in the race timezone; honest official tracking availability; and an official results link sourced from event data.

## Non-goals

- No generalized race-links model.
- No live-results ingestion or tracking integration.
- No changes outside the race import and page slice.

## Requirements

- Persist race timezone and `results_url` from event YAML.
- Derive key clock times from the race schedule and turnaround station cutoff.
- Render weekday, clock time, and timezone abbreviation.
- Link tracking/results only when their URLs are present; otherwise show clear availability copy.
- Keep repeat imports idempotent.

## Design and likely files

- `db/migrate/*_add_follow_fields_to_races.rb`, `db/schema.rb`: race timezone and results URL.
- `db/events/bighorn-100.yml`: America/Denver timezone and verified official results URL.
- `app/services/events/import.rb`: existing race attribute import remains the boundary.
- `app/models/race.rb`: race-local schedule instants derived from race/station facts.
- `app/helpers/application_helper.rb`: key-clock-time formatting.
- `app/views/races/show.html.erb`: Follow timing strip and link cards.
- Import/page tests and changelog.
