# TUR-11: Crew & pacers section

## Objective

Complete the event-generic Crew & pacers section with sourced pacer rules, a crew-route summary, and links that activate the existing terrain-map crew layer and station-table crew filter.

## Non-goals

- No second crew table or map.
- No new crew-route generation or source research.
- No mobile redesign or broader race-page polish.

## Requirements

- Render sourced pacer rules with an honest missing-data fallback.
- Show the crew route label, total distance, and total drive hours from current race data.
- "Show drive route on map" jumps to Course and enables the crew layer.
- "Crew-accessible passes" jumps to Aid stations and applies the Crew access filter.
- Keep the existing map and master station-pass table as the only full surfaces.

## Design and likely files

- `app/views/races/show.html.erb`: crew cards, links, shared controller scope.
- `app/views/races/_map.html.erb`: externally addressable crew-layer control.
- `app/views/races/_aid_station_table.html.erb`: shared filter controller scope.
- `app/javascript/controllers/race_actions_controller.js`: cross-section event dispatch and navigation.
- Existing map/filter controllers: locally owned public actions for selecting crew data.
- Controller and system tests at the approved public seams.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: user-visible behavior.
